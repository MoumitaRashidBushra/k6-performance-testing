Project 2: EasyPay
Feature 1: Merchant and Utility Bill Payment
An EasyPay user can pay merchant and utility bills through the application.

For merchant payments, the system deducts a 1% service charge, with a minimum charge of 5 TK. If the merchant payment amount is more than 5,000 TK, the user receives 10% cashback. If the amount is more than 10,000 TK, the user receives 20% cashback, with a maximum cashback limit of 3,000 TK.

For utility bill payments, no cashback is provided regardless of the transaction amount.

Students must analyse the feature and write the acceptance criteria in bullet points.

Feature 2: Loan Management
An EasyPay user with a balance below 100 TK can apply for a loan of up to 20,000 TK.

If the loan is fully repaid within 30 days from the loan initiation date, no interest is charged. After 30 days, the system applies 1.8% daily compound interest to the remaining unpaid amount.

A user becomes eligible to apply for another loan after repaying at least 50% of the outstanding loan amount.
